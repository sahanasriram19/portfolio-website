UI/UX case study

AI Study Buddy

A mobile learning companion that brings study planning, focus sessions, AI help, practice and progress tracking into one app. These are the Figma wireframes and interaction flows behind it.

Role
UI/UX designer
Tool

Wireframes and flows
See the screens

One study loop, four steps

Every screen in the app belongs to one of these steps, and each step feeds the next.

Plan

Organise tasks, with an AI breakdown that turns a big task into smaller ones.

Focus

Run a timed focus session with distraction controls, then see a session-complete summary.

Practise

Build study kits and test yourself with quizzes, flashcards and mock exams.

Review

Check scores, statistics, achievements and leaderboards, then plan the next round.

A five-tab bar (Home, Tasks, Study, AI Help, Progress) runs across the main screens, so the loop stays one tap away from anywhere.

What went into the design

Study organisation

Task screens, AI task breakdown, focus timers, distraction controls and session-completion views.

Learning and AI tools

Study kits, practice questions, flashcards, mock exams, AI chat, and audio and image interactions.

Progress and personalisation

Score feedback, statistics, leaderboards, achievement screens and editable profile preferences.

Empty, error and confirm states

Designed beyond the happy path: a login error, an empty study-kit list, a form validation error, and confirmation dialogs before logging out, submitting an exam or discarding changes.

Takeaway

Translated a broad set of study and AI-assisted workflows into a coherent mobile UI/UX prototype.

The screens

Four Figma boards cover the whole app. Select a board to open it, then select the image again to switch between fit-to-screen and full size.
