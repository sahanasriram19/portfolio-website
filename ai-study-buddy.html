AI Study Buddy

A mobile study app I designed in Figma. The wireframes cover planning tasks, focus sessions, AI help, practice and progress.

  • TypeConcept and wireframes
  • ToolFigma
  • PlatformMobile
  • RoleUI/UX design

How the app fits together

Plan

Organise tasks, with an AI breakdown for big ones.

Focus

A timed focus session with app blocking, then a session summary.

Practise

Study kits with quizzes, flashcards and mock exams.

Review

Scores, statistics, achievements and leaderboards.

A tab bar (Home, Tasks, Study, AI Help, Progress) sits across the main screens, so each step is one tap away.

What is in the designs

Study organisation

Task screens, the AI task breakdown, focus timers, app blocking and session complete views.

Learning and AI tools

Study kits, practice questions, flashcards, mock exams, AI chat, audio and image tools.

Progress and personalisation

Score feedback, statistics, leaderboards, achievements and editable profile settings.

Empty, error and confirm states

A login error, an empty study kit list, a form error, and dialogs before logging out, submitting an exam or discarding changes.

I wanted the screens to cover what happens when things go wrong, not only the main path.
