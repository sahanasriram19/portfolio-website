MoodTunes

A music journal that connects to Spotify. You pick a mood, log a song with a note about how you feel, and the app builds playlists and listening sessions from what you have logged.

  • TypePersonal project
  • Built withNode.js, Express, MySQL, vanilla JS
  • APIsSpotify, Last.fm
  • LoginJWT and bcrypt
Live demo ↗ GitHub ↗

How it works

Log a song

Pick a mood, search Spotify and save the song with a short note.

Get playlists

Songs are grouped by mood. One click syncs a mood to a playlist in your Spotify account.

Start a session

Picks come from the genres of the artists you have logged for that mood. Premium accounts get them queued, Free accounts get a playlist.

Look back

History with filters, plus a monthly recap you can save as an image.

What is in it

Journal

Seven moods, song search, personal notes, and play counts that sync from Spotify when you come back.

Playlists and sessions

Mood playlists, Spotify sync, and sessions with recommendations from Spotify genres. Last.fm tags fill in when Spotify has none.

Stats and reminders

A history timeline, a stats page, the monthly recap and an optional daily check-in notification.

Backend

An Express API with a MySQL database, Spotify OAuth, and accounts protected with JWT and bcrypt.

I built this full application on my own, from the database to the Spotify login.
