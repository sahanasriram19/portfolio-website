MoodTunes

A music mood journal that connects to your Spotify account. Log songs by how they make you feel, build mood-based playlists, run listening sessions and look back on your listening through stats and monthly recaps.

Type
Personal project
Stack
Node.js, Express, MySQL
Integrations

Status
Live
Live demo ↗ GitHub ↗

From mood to insight

The app follows one loop: feel something, log it, listen again, then look back at the pattern.

Choose a mood

Pick happy, sad, hype, heartbreak, nostalgic, focused or chill, then search Spotify and log a song with a note.

Build playlists

Songs group into mood playlists that sync to a real playlist in your Spotify account in one click.

Run a session

Start a listening session. Genre-based picks from Spotify and Last.fm are queued, or saved as a playlist on Free accounts.

Explore trends

History, filters and a shareable monthly recap show your top mood, top songs and busiest day.

Under the hood: Spotify OAuth and Last.fm feed an Express API, which stores logs and sessions in MySQL and serves a vanilla JavaScript interface.

What went into it

Mood journal

Log Spotify songs under moods, add personal reflections, and sync recently played tracks and play counts.

Playlists and sessions

Build mood playlists, sync them to Spotify, and run sessions with genre-based picks informed by Spotify and Last.fm.

Insights and reminders

Session history, listening filters, shareable monthly recaps and optional daily push reminders.

Full-stack build

Vanilla JavaScript, HTML/CSS, Node.js and Express, MySQL, Spotify OAuth, JWT and bcrypt, plus external music APIs.

Takeaway

An independently built music app combining personal storytelling, secure accounts, live APIs and useful listening data.
