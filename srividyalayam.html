From client brief to live site

Content came first: the design grew out of what the school needed to say.

School identity

Music and tradition set the tone, using the client’s own photography, colours and ornament.

Content design

The founder’s story, the classes on offer and the teaching philosophy shaped each page.

Responsive UI

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with a shared navigation and layouts that work on a phone.

Enrolment

A clear call to action on every page leads to an online enquiry form.

The visitor journey: find a class, meet the founder, view the gallery, then enrol online.

What went into it

Commissioned work

Built around the client’s photography, identity, teaching philosophy and requirements.

Informative pages

Presents the school, founder, Carnatic music classes and gallery, with clear calls to action throughout.

Enrolment experience

A form for student and guardian details, class interests, experience level and availability.

Frontend execution

Responsive HTML/CSS/vanilla JavaScript, reusable shared scripts, active navigation, a gallery and scroll reveals.

Takeaway

Blended content, design and practical frontend work into a real client-facing music-school experience.
